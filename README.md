## Towards a soft three-level voting model (Soft T-LVM) for fake news detection

**Boutheina Jlifi, Chayma Sakrani, and Claude Duvallet** · *Journal of Intelligent Information Systems*, 2023

This paper introduces **Soft T-LVM**, a three-level ensemble for detecting COVID-19 misinformation. The model represents each news item in six ways using Bag-of-Words and TF-IDF with unigrams, bigrams, and trigrams. It combines classifier probabilities within each representation, merges the results within each feature family, and then combines the two families for the final prediction. The paper reports **94.43% accuracy** on its evaluation dataset.

**Interactive simulation.** Enter a news item, explore all six feature charts, and select an n-gram to see where it appears in the text and how its count or TF-IDF weight is calculated. You can then follow illustrative classifier scores through the three voting levels and adjust the final voting weights and decision threshold.
